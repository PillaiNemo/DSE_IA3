How EduGuard keeps student support fair, private and in human hands.

Our principles

A flag is an invitation to help, never a verdict.

1People decideEduGuard only suggests. A counsellor always reviews and decides what happens.
2Private by designRecords are anonymised and stay in the browser. Alerts are drafts, never sent automatically.
3Checked for fairnessResults are compared across groups so no group is overlooked.
4Always explainedEvery score shows the reasons behind it, in plain words.

How to use EduGuard.

Use it to…

  • Decide which students to contact first.
  • Start a supportive conversation with a student.
  • Plan group support such as tutoring or fee-help sessions.
  • Prepare for a meeting with a one-page brief.

Never use it to…

  • Grade, rank or discipline a student.
  • Make admission or scholarship decisions.
  • Share a student's risk with classmates or the public.
  • Replace a conversation with the student.

Limitations to keep in mind.

Built on past recordsLearned from one university's past students; patterns elsewhere may differ.
Mid-course warningWorks best once semester results are in, not at admission.
Some false alarmsSome flagged students would have been fine; a short check-in clears this up.
Guidelines, not rulesSupport suggestions are starting points; adapt them to each student.
